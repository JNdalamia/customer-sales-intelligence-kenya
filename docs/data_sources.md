# Data Inventory

This document lists the data I have for the project and what each part is for.

## Data included now

|---|---|---|---|---|

| `sales_lines_synthetic.csv` | One product line on a receipt | Product sales, units, discounts and estimated gross profit | Generated by my project script; all records are fictional | Prices and costs are made up for practice. |

## Notes about source terms

- I record the exact source link and the date I checked it.
- I check the licence for the specific report or spreadsheet, rather than assuming every file on a website has the same terms.
- The current KNBS CPI source says CC BY-NC 4.0. I will give credit to KNBS and respect the non-commercial condition.
- The business tables are generated by my script. They are not actual sales or customer records.
- I will not invent CPI or holiday data to fill gaps.
